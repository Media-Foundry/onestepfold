## Decisions
- Use PDB/mmCIF experimental coordinates as GT; UniProt/SIFTS are provenance and traceability only.
- Keep the universal GT schema multi-chain-aware, but use a monomer-first training view: exactly one generated protein chain instance, no nucleic-acid or other-polymer chain instances, primary X-ray/EM/neutron method, one coordinate model, length 20--1024.

- Store exact 100% sequence identity grouping only. Preserve near-identical constructs and small mutations; keep each exact group in one split.
